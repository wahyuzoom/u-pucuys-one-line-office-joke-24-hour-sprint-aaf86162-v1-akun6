# One-Line Office Joke: 24-Hour Sprint — Entry

One short joke about office life, at most 200 characters, as a
single line.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`.
Stdlib only, offline, deterministic.

## The joke

> I asked HR for a raise and they gave me a mindfulness app. Now I'm calmly underpaid.

84 characters, one line.
